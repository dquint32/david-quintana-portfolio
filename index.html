Organized 10 years of cancer-research records

Organicé 10 años de registros de investigación oncológica

A cancer research network had a decade of clinical-trial documents spread across inconsistently named folders, plus an old patient database from a system that had been shut down. Staff struggled to find documents and to track protocol problems.

I wrote a step-by-step program that listed and sorted every file, cleaned up the patient database, and checked that each document matched a real patient. That check showed that nearly 1 in 5 patient IDs didn't match, and we caught it before anything was moved. I then built a searchable SharePoint archive for the team. Patient data never left the organization's computers.

Una red de investigación oncológica tenía una década de documentos de ensayos clínicos repartidos en carpetas con nombres inconsistentes, además de una base de datos de pacientes de un sistema ya dado de baja. Al personal le costaba encontrar documentos y dar seguimiento a problemas de protocolo.

Escribí un programa por etapas que listó y clasificó cada archivo, limpió la base de datos de pacientes y verificó que cada documento correspondiera a un paciente real. Esa verificación mostró que casi 1 de cada 5 IDs de paciente no coincidía, y lo detectamos antes de mover nada. Después construí un archivo en SharePoint con búsqueda para el equipo. Los datos de pacientes nunca salieron de las computadoras de la organización.

Sobre mí

I'm David Quintana, a bilingual Health Care Information Systems specialist and clinical data engineer, and a 2026 B.S. HCIS graduate of MSU Denver.

Clinical data engineering. As an intern at Western States Cancer Research NCORP, I engineered Python/pandas ETL pipelines to extract, clean, and transition legacy clinical-trial records into structured, compliant SharePoint data architectures — with hands-on experience in ETL workflows, HL7 FHIR R4, EHR/EMR transitions, and HIPAA-compliant records management.

Regulatory & clinical-research fluency. I'm CITI-certified in Good Clinical Practice (US FDA), human-subjects research, HIPAA for research, and clinical research fundamentals, with working knowledge of IRB process, protocol deviations, and ICH-GCP record integrity.

Bilingual by design. As founder of Servicios Quintana LLC and a commissioned Colorado Notary Public, I've spent years preparing and auditing regulated healthcare and legal documents in English and Spanish — because a compliance gap and a language gap are so often the same gap.
