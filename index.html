About Me

I'm David Quintana — a Health Care Information Systems professional focused on where clinical data, regulation, and patient safety converge. My work rests on three reinforcing pillars.

Clinical data mastery: I work directly with HL7 v2.x and FHIR R4, engineering the parsing, validation, and migration logic that turns messy legacy data into structured, interoperable, audit‑ready records. At Western States Cancer Research NCORP, that means safeguarding thousands of trial documents and patient records through a controlled, HIPAA‑compliant migration.

Regulatory fluency: I hold FDA Good Clinical Practice (GCP), HIPAA, and human‑subjects certifications through the CITI Program, and I'm beginning a Paralegal Certificate with a medical‑legal focus. Bilingual protection: as founder of Servicios Quintana LLC and a commissioned Colorado Notary Public, I've spent a decade preparing, translating, and auditing regulated healthcare and legal documents — because a compliance gap and a language gap are so often the same gap.
