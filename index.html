About

Sobre mí

I'm David Quintana, a bilingual Health Care Information Systems specialist and clinical data engineer, and a 2026 B.S. HCIS graduate of MSU Denver.

Clinical data engineering. As an intern at Western States Cancer Research NCORP, I engineered Python/pandas ETL pipelines to extract, clean, and transition legacy clinical-trial records into structured, compliant SharePoint data architectures — with hands-on experience in ETL workflows, HL7 FHIR R4, EHR/EMR transitions, and HIPAA-compliant records management.

Regulatory & clinical-research fluency. I'm CITI-certified in Good Clinical Practice (US FDA), human-subjects research, HIPAA for research, and clinical research fundamentals, with working knowledge of IRB process, protocol deviations, and ICH-GCP record integrity.

Bilingual by design. As founder of Servicios Quintana LLC and a commissioned Colorado Notary Public, I've spent years preparing and auditing regulated healthcare and legal documents in English and Spanish — because a compliance gap and a language gap are so often the same gap.

Soy David Quintana, especialista bilingüe en Sistemas de Información de Salud e ingeniero de datos clínicos, graduado en 2026 de la Licenciatura en HCIS de MSU Denver.

Fluidez regulatoria y de investigación clínica.
