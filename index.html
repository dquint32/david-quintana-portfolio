Servicios Quintana LLC

Corporate Infrastructure & Multi-Brand Strategy

A high-performance parent company portal designed to unify disparate service brands under a single, authoritative digital identity.

  • Technical Achievement: Engineered a Neo-Brutalist Latino Tech design system featuring a centralized, lightweight i18n framework that manages global brand messaging through a singular `translations` object. This architecture utilizes Immediately Invoked Function Expressions (IIFE) for modularity and a robust Scroll Reveal engine that dynamically triggers animations based on user viewport intersection. Additionally, implemented a custom, high-contrast cursor system and Reduced Motion logic to ensure the site remains WCAG 2.1 AA compliant for users with vestibular sensitivities.
  • Impact: Established a professional "Corporate HQ" that bridges the gap between community-focused services and legal compliance. By centralizing three distinct brands (Quintana Notary, Ayuda DMV, and David Quintana Dev) into a unified hub, the platform projects institutional authority and trust. The design uses visual separators and hard-shadow "Neo-Brutalist" cards to create clear information hierarchy, making complex corporate structures immediately scannable for both potential business partners and local clients.
  • Stack: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+, IIFE Modules), HTML5 (Semantic & ARIA-compliant), CSS3 (Custom Variables, Flexbox, Grid), FontAwesome, Google Fonts, and specialized WCAG accessibility triggers.

Tienda Salvadoreña

Digital Preservation of a 30-Year Legacy

Digitized Denver's oldest Salvadoran grocery store with a focus on cultural identity.

  • Technical Achievement: Engineered a custom, object-oriented translation engine in Vanilla JavaScript using a dictionary-based key system. This system allows for instantaneous switching between English and Spanish without page reloads or external libraries. It features a robust "Critical Fix" architecture that handles nested data-attributes and ensures language persistence across a multi-page site through browser session management.
  • Impact: Digitized and preserved the online presence of Denver's oldest Salvadoran grocery store, a 30-year community pillar. By implementing a bilingual UX, the platform connects the older "diaspora" generation with younger English-dominant family members, effectively modernizing the business's reach while honoring its cultural heritage through nostalgic visual storytelling (Pollo Campero and Family History sections).
  • Stack: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6), HTML5 (Semantic & ARIA-compliant), CSS3 (Custom Variables, Flexbox, Grid), Google Maps API, and AI-assisted visual asset generation.

Tienda Salvadoreña

Preservación Digital de un Legado de 30 Años

Quintana Notary & Signing

Legal Authority and Mobile Conversion

Active professional platform for bilingual notary services, designed to build trust and capture clients.
