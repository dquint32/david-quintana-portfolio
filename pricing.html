Tienda Salvadoreña — Denver

Digital Preservation of a 30‑Year Legacy

Digitized Denver's oldest Salvadoran grocery store with a focus on cultural identity and bilingual UX.

Technical Achievement:

Built a custom, object‑oriented translation engine in Vanilla JS using a dictionary‑key system for instant bilingual switching without reloads. Implemented a "Critical Fix" architecture for nested data attributes and session‑based language persistence.

Impact:

Preserved the digital presence of a 30‑year community landmark. The bilingual UX connects older diaspora members with younger English‑dominant generations through nostalgic visual storytelling.

Stack:
Vanilla JavaScript (ES6), Semantic HTML5, CSS3 (Variables, Flexbox, Grid), Google Maps API.

Preservación Digital de un Legado de 30 Años
